Holistic Development Model

Our Academic Curriculum & Daily Rhythm

A structured yet joyful balance of inquiry-based exploration, bilingual immersion, and creative physical activities.

Bilingual Immersion

English and Kazakh language integration with native educators during play, mealtime, and storytelling.

Early STEM & Logic

Lego Education Coding Express, interactive math games, and nature-based scientific observations.

Sensory & Arts Studio

Clay sculpting, watercolor, musical rhythm, and tactile materials that foster fine motor control.

Social & Emotional (SEL)

Conflict resolution, empathy circles, mindfulness breathing, and team problem-solving skills.

Structured Daily Flow

Full-Day Kindergarten Routine

Consistent schedules create security and emotional well-being while allowing ample room for spontaneous child-led discovery.
