Our Philosophy

A Safe Space Where Every Child Flourishes Naturally

At Astana Early Academy, education is not about memorization—it is about nurturing curiosity and resilience. Our bespoke pedagogy blends Montessori exploratory principles with the Reggio Emilia project approach.

  • Bilingual Immersion (English & Kazakh/Russian) Native educators guide language acquisition through natural daily conversations and storytelling.
  • Eco-Friendly Architecture & Sensory Gardens Constructed with non-toxic natural wood, HEPA air filtration, and direct access to green outdoor play spaces.
  • Early STEM & Creative Arts Studio Hands-on robotics blocks, sensory play, painting, music, and junior scientific experiments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about our daily routines, dietary safety, and enrollment steps.

We maintain strict, safety-certified ratios: 1:6 for Toddlers (ages 1.5–3), 1:8 for Preschool (ages 3–5), and 1:10 for Pre-K (ages 5–6). Each group has both a lead licensed pedagogue and an assistant educator at all times.

All meals are prepared fresh daily in our on-campus professional kitchen by certified chefs. We cater individually to lactose intolerance, gluten allergies, vegetarian, and Halal-compliant dietary requirements.
